Executive Summary
EasyPG is a multi-tenant PG/Hostel management SaaS built with a modern stack (Next. js 15 + Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL + BULLMQ/Redis). It targets property owners managing

multiple branches with hierarchical RBAC (SuperAdmin ~ Owner ~ Warden). The codebase shows strong architectural foundations but has critical production gaps that need addressing
before scaling.

WW FINAL NOTES

The team has built a genuinely impressive domain model for a complex multi-tenant PG management system. The Prisma schema alone puts this in the top quartile of SaaS MVPs. The mail
blocker is operational maturity - testing, security hygiene, and observability.

Recommendation: Spend 2 weeks on P0/P1 items before any customer-facing launch. The foundation is solid; the scaffolding needs hardening.

Review conducted as Senior SaaS Architecture Consultant. No code was modified during this analysis.
